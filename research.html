RESEARCH / FOUNDER BACKGROUND

A researcher’s eye for the cosmos.

Supriyo Saha is a PhD researcher in cosmology at TIFR Mumbai. That work informs the care we bring to communicating science.

THE FIELD

How did the universe become what we see?

Cosmology studies the history and structure of the universe using observations, mathematical models, and physical theory. Questions about the early universe and dark matter are part of this larger search.

This page describes Supriyo’s research field. Specific papers, discoveries, and institutional projects will be listed only when verified and approved for publication.

Our communication work ranges beyond cosmology—from biology to AI—while keeping the same habits of clarity, evidence, and curiosity.
