FUTURE SCIENTISTS / PROGRAMME VISION

Give curiosity a method.

Future Scientists is a developing programme idea for students who want to go beyond memorising answers and learn how to investigate questions.

“A future scientist is not someone who knows everything. It is someone who knows how to ask, test, and keep learning.”

THE APPROACH

From wonder to investigation.

01 · Ask

Turn a broad curiosity into a question that can be explored.

02 · Test

Use observation, models, and simple experiments to compare ideas.

03 · Explain

Communicate what the evidence says, what it does not, and what comes next.

WHO IT IS FOR

A pathway for curious students.

The programme is being shaped for school-age learners and educators. Format, duration, and delivery can be developed with partner schools; no fixed cohort or outcome is claimed yet.
