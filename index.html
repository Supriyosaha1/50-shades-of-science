INDEPENDENT SCIENCE COMMUNICATION

Bringing Science Out of the Lab.

50 Shades of Science was built on a simple conviction: the universe is astonishing, but traditional science education often forgets to share the excitement. We create dynamic visual films, interactive classroom sessions, and public science experiences that make complex phenomena tangible.

Founded by astrophysicist and communicator Supriyo Saha (research scholar at TIFR Mumbai), the initiative combines active scientific rigor with relatable, conversational storytelling. We don't just lecture answers—we guide audiences through how discoveries are actually made.

WHAT WE DELIVER
  • Visual Films & Reels: Crisp, high-production explainers on breakthrough science & Nobel prizes.
  • Interactive School Talks: Question-led sessions tailored for grades 8–12 that ignite curiosity.
  • Public Science Events: Engaging bar talks, science days, and discussions for general audiences.
